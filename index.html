FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Open a tool and use it.

Is it really free?

Yes. All 7 tools are free to use.

Is my information saved or uploaded?

Most tools run in your browser, so your data stays on your device. The Class Scheduler sends your photo to an AI reader only to read your classes. It is not stored.

Do I need an internet connection?

The pages need internet to load. The Class Scheduler needs it to read your photo. The other tools run in your browser once the page has loaded.

Which devices are supported?

Phones, tablets and laptops. The pages adjust to your screen size, and the tools work in the browser.
