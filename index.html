FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Open a tool and use it.

Is it really free?

Yes. All 7 tools are free to use.

Is my information saved or uploaded?

Most tools work inside your own browser, so your data stays on your device. The Class Scheduler sends your photo to an AI reader only to read your classes. It is not stored.

Which devices does it work on?

Phones, tablets and laptops. The pages adjust to your screen size.

Who made Study Hub?

A group of BS Information Technology students (SBIT-1F) at QCU San Bartolome Campus, as a Web Systems final project. You can meet everyone on the Team page.
